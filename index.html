FAQ

Common questions

Does my data leave our systems?

No. DockPulse runs entirely in your browser — files are parsed locally and nothing is uploaded to a server.

Which WMS systems are supported?

Any system that can export to CSV or Excel. DockPulse auto-detects common column names for receiving, shipping, orders, and labor reports, and lets you correct the mapping if it guesses wrong.

Do I need to combine my reports myself first?

No — upload your receiving, shipping, and labor reports separately and DockPulse merges them by date automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract. Cancel anytime and you won't be billed for the next cycle.

Is the 50% off first month real?

Yes — every new account gets 50% off its first month on either plan, so you can see real KPIs from your own data at half the cost before committing. Cancel any time and you won't be billed further.
